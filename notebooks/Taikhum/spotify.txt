
Title : Design and develop a cloud-based Global Music Intelligence Platform that leverages Big Data and Machine Learning to analyze streaming behavior, predict music trends, identify emerging artists, evaluate label performance, and support strategic decision-making in the music industry.


Problem Statement : The music streaming industry generates massive amounts of data from songs, artists, albums, and user listening activities across multiple countries. However, extracting meaningful insights, identifying emerging artists, predicting hit songs, and understanding global music trends remain challenging due to the scale and complexity of the data.
This project aims to develop a Global Music Intelligence Platform to analyze Spotify streaming data, discover market trends, evaluate artist and label performance, identify profitable music markets, and predict future song success to provide scalable analytics and data-driven decision support for the music industry.

Why this is a real-world problem
It directly addresses:
Record labels wasting money on the wrong artists.
Difficulty identifying future stars before competitors.
Challenges in predicting whether a song will become a hit.
Inefficient marketing campaign allocation.
Lack of visibility into emerging music markets.
High uncertainty in artist acquisition and promotion decisions.

Inferences after EDA : 
1] Music Consumption is Highly Concentrated : A small group of artists and songs generate a disproportionately large share of total streams.
2] Global Artists Have Significant Competitive Advantage : Artists such as Drake, BTS, Ed Sheeran, Justin Bieber, etc. appear across many countries.
3] Evergreen Songs Continue Generating Revenue : Several older songs continue receiving streams years after release.
4] Chart Longevity Matters More Than Viral Popularity : Some songs never achieve Rank 1 but remain on charts for long periods.
5] Certain Countries Have Diverse Music Preferences : Countries differ significantly in artist and song diversity.
6] Market Saturation Differs Across Countries : Some countries contain a very large number of competing artists and songs.
7] Record Labels Show Uneven Performance : A few labels dominate total streams while others have limited market impact.
8] Some Labels Depend Heavily on Single Artists : Certain labels generate most streams from one or two artists.
9] Album Success is Influenced by Longevity : Albums remaining on charts longer usually outperform short-lived albums.
10] International Reach Drives Streaming Power : Artists present in many countries usually generate significantly higher streams.


Feature Engineering : 
1] Artist Export Score – Measures an artist's global reach based on streams and number of countries.
2] Evergreen Song Index – Identifies songs that remain popular over a long period.
3] Artist Sustainability Score – Evaluates long-term artist success using chart longevity and peak rank.
4] Album Longevity Score – Measures album performance based on weeks on chart and ranking.
5] Market Saturation Index – Indicates competition level within a country.
6] Music Diversity Score – Measures the variety of songs and artists consumed in a market.
7] Collaboration Indicator – Categorizes songs as solo or collaborative tracks.
8] Song Age – Calculates the age of a song from its release date.
9] Global Reach Features – Number of countries where a song, artist, or album appears.
10] Hit Score – Composite metric created using streams, rankings, chart duration, and geographic reach to support hit prediction.
